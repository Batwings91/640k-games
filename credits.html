Third-party art and music

We only use assets under licences that allow commercial use: CC0, CC-BY and CC-BY-SA. We never use non-commercial licences, brand names, or characters and art from real games.

The licences, briefly

CC0
Public domain dedication. No attribution required, but we credit anyway.
CC-BY 3.0
Free to use and modify, with credit to the author. Full text
CC-BY-SA 3.0
As CC-BY, and anything derived from it must be shared under the same licence. Our modified sprites derived from CC-BY-SA packs are therefore also CC-BY-SA. This does not affect the game code. Full text

Where we have modified an asset, the game's credits say how (resized, palette-reduced, recoloured, and so on), as the licences ask.

Contributors we rely on

Most of our assets come from OpenGameArt.org. Across our games so far, thanks are due to Skorpio, Daniel Cook (Lostgarden), chabull, LuminousDragonGames, Kenney, and Alexandr Zhelanov. Links to their work are on the game pages.

Using our CC-BY-SA sprites

The modified ship and alien sprites in Spore Wars are released under CC-BY-SA 3.0, as the licence requires. If you want them, credit both the original author (Skorpio) and 640K Games, and share your derivatives under the same licence. Everything else in the games is not available for reuse.
