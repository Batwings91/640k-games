Spore Wars

A vertical-scrolling shoot-'em-up in the mid-90s PC style. Climb five gun levels, buy shields and upgrades with salvaged cores, and face a boss every fifth wave.

Play

Spore Wars will be free to play in the browser on game portals. Nothing to install.

What's in it

  • Five weapon levels with distinct hardware, homing support rockets at level four, and a megabomb for emergencies
  • A three-hit hull, shields, repair pickups and a kill-chain score multiplier
  • Three sectors, from the Orbital Foundry through Infected Salvage to the Spore Heart
  • Rotating bosses every fifth wave: the Battleship, the Assault Mech and the Brood Mother
  • Cores salvaged in combat buy permanent upgrades from an alien trader between sectors
  • Sound effects synthesised to sound like an early-90s sound card, and a CC0 music loop that shifts with each sector
