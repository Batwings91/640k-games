Spore Wars

A vertical-scrolling shoot-'em-up in the mid-90s PC style. Climb five gun levels, buy shields and upgrades with salvaged cores, and face a boss every fifth wave.

Play

Spore Wars will be free to play in the browser on game portals. Nothing to install.

▶ Play on itch.io — coming soon

Planned rollout: itch.io first, then CrazyGames and Poki. Links appear here as each goes live.

Readme

Genre
Vertical shoot-'em-up, endless waves with upgrades
Platform
Browser (desktop and phone), keyboard or touch
Price
Free, supported by portal ads
Status
In development. First release on itch.io.
Made with
Plain HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript, no engine

What's in it

  • Five weapon levels with distinct projectiles, plus a megabomb for emergencies
  • Shields, cores and a workshop for buying upgrades between runs
  • Five enemy types and rotating bosses: a Battleship, a Mech and a Mothership
  • Six-layer parallax starfield and a streamed music track
  • Sound effects synthesised to sound like an early-90s sound card
